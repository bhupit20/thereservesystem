Book promise

Not immortality. Margin engineering.

This is a book about the decades before the ICU, the quiet patterns that determine whether a body bends or breaks, and the sequence required to build real longevity.

The framework is practical because the stakes are clinical. Reserve determines whether medicine has something to work with.
